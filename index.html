FAQs

What does a SaaS build cost?

Focused MVPs start from £5,000. Larger or more complex products typically cost £10,000 or more. We agree the scope and provide a fixed quote before work begins.

How long will my build take?

An agreed MVP build takes two weeks from a ready-to-build start: the scope is confirmed, the deposit is paid and the necessary access and materials are supplied. Client delays extend the delivery date. Larger products have a timeline agreed for their scope.

Can you build on what I already have?

Yes. We review the existing product and codebase before scoping an extension or rebuild. We can also work alongside your developers on an agreed part of the product.

Do I need investment or paying customers?

No. You can come to us with a product idea or an existing SaaS. We will work through the scope and budget needed to build it.

Will I own the code?

Yes. At handover, you receive the source code, deployment access and information needed to run and develop your product.

What happens after launch?

Optional ongoing support is available through a monthly retainer, priced around your product and the support required. Larger additions and new features are scoped separately.

What happens on the first call?

We talk through your idea or existing product, what you need built and your intended timing. From there, we agree the next step towards a scope and quote.
